Refund Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Subscription purchases

Sales Research Pro subscriptions are sold through Paddle, which acts as our Merchant of Record and handles payment, billing and eligible refund processing.

Refund requests

If you believe a charge should be refunded, contact support@salesresearchpro.com with the purchase email and relevant transaction details. Refund requests may also be handled through Paddle's buyer-support processes.

Eligibility

Refunds are assessed under applicable law, Paddle's applicable policies and the circumstances of the transaction. Mandatory consumer cancellation, withdrawal or refund rights are not limited by this policy.

Cancellation versus refund

Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals but does not automatically reverse a completed charge. Unless a refund or mandatory legal remedy applies, access may continue through the paid subscription period.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

Contact support promptly if you believe you were charged more than once or charged incorrectly so the transaction can be investigated with Paddle.

Processing

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment flow. Timing depends on Paddle, the payment method and your financial institution.
