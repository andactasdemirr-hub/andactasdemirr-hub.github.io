Questions

Good to know.

Does Sales Research Pro guarantee that a product will be profitable?

No. It is a research and qualification tool. Results are signals for further evaluation, not a guarantee of sales, margin or profitability.

Why can a product sometimes require another verification step?

Some marketplace research data depends on an authenticated session and the availability of the marketplace's own research tools. If the marketplace asks for sign-in or verification, the extension stops rather than attempting to bypass it.

Can I cancel a monthly subscription?

Yes. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Billing is handled through Paddle.
