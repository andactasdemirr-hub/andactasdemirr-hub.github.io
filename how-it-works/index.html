HOW IT WORKS

Five clear steps. One research workflow.

These illustrations show the steps and controls, not live marketplace data.

01 / EBAY

Open the seller’s Sold Items page

Visit the seller on eBay and select Sold Items. Do not copy the active store inventory URL. The Sold Items results link often contains LH_Sold=1.

Seller store · Active listings

02 / EBAY

Choose 60, 120 or 240 items

If eBay offers a results-per-page selector, choose 60, 120 or 240 items. This is done on eBay, not in the extension. Available choices may differ by page or account.

03 / EXTENSION

Paste the link and configure filters

Paste the Sold Items URL into the Stores field. Set Minimum Sold, Recent Days and Min Purchases. Hover over the help icons to understand each field, then start scanning.

04 / RESEARCH

Review qualified results

Watch the progress and review products that meet your configured sales criteria. Check the underlying marketplace information yourself; results are research signals, not guaranteed sales or profit.

05 / AMAZON FINDER

Compare Amazon research alternatives

Open Amazon Finder to review candidate products, estimated margin and other filters. Brand, variant and pack quantity may differ because these are research alternatives, not verified identical products. Confirm price, availability, fees and suitability manually.

AMAZON FINDER
✓ Product relevance
✓ Estimated opportunity margin
✓ Manual verification before listing
Important: Use the Sold Items URL rather than the seller inventory URL. Never assume a candidate Amazon product is identical, in stock or profitable without checking.
